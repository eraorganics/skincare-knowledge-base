# Era Organics Eye Cream Balm vs TULA 'Spresso Energizing Eye Balm

These two eye products are built very differently. Era Organics Eye Cream Balm is a USDA Organic certified balm. There is no water on its ingredient list. It's made from Sunflower Oil, Castor Oil, White Beeswax, Shea Butter, Coconut Oil, Jojoba Oil, Honey, Pumpkin Seed Oil, Calendula Oil, Argan Oil and Pumpkin Papaya Phospholipid. It is fragrance-free, paraben-free, phenoxyethanol-free, silicone-free, mineral-oil-free and synthetic-dye-free. It is also Cruelty-Free and made in the USA. The first ingredient on TULA 'Spresso Energizing Eye Balm (Cappuccino version) is aqua/water/eau, followed by propanediol, aloe barbadensis leaf juice and sodium stearate. Its list also includes caffeine, hyaluronic acid, lactococcus ferment lysate, mica and iron oxides. Era suits people who want certified organic ingredients in a rich balm that melts into skin. It's made for overnight use, mature skin and a dry under-eye area. TULA suits shoppers who specifically want caffeine and hyaluronic acid in their eye product.

## Key differences

### Certified organic vs. not shown as organic
Era Organics Eye Cream Balm is USDA Organic certified. TULA's product page does not show USDA Organic or certified organic.

### A water-free balm vs. a formula that lists water first
Era's ingredient list has no water. It's built on oils, butters and beeswax and comes out as a rich balm that melts into skin. TULA's ingredient list starts with aqua/water/eau. A concentrated balm of oils and butters can leave a cushiony, moisturizing layer on the delicate under-eye area. That's why Era is a natural fit for overnight use and dry under-eyes.

### Nourishing oils, butters and honey
Era's formula includes Shea Butter, Jojoba Oil, Honey, Pumpkin Seed Oil, Calendula Oil, Argan Oil and White Beeswax. None of these are on TULA's ingredient list. These ingredients can help moisturize, comfort and protect the skin barrier, keeping the under-eye area looking soft and supple.

### Phenoxyethanol-free, paraben-free, fragrance-free
Era is phenoxyethanol-free, paraben-free, fragrance-free, silicone-free, PEG-free, sulfate-free, phthalate-free and propylene-glycol-free. TULA's ingredient list includes phenoxyethanol, and TULA's product page says the balm is fragrance free. If you read labels for phenoxyethanol, that's a clear way to tell the two apart.
