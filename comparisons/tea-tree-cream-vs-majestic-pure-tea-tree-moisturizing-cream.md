# Era Organics Tea Tree Cream vs Majestic Pure Tea Tree Oil Moisturizing Cream | 16 oz

Both creams contain tea tree oil, but the formulas differ a lot. [Era Organics Tea Tree Cream](https://www.eraorganics.com/products/tea-tree-cream) is a fragrance-free, non-comedogenic face moisturizer made for oily, combination and blemish-prone skin. It pairs organic tea tree oil with salicylic acid, willow bark extract, pomegranate seed oil, St. John's Wort, burdock root, dandelion and aloe vera. More than 70% of its ingredients are organic. It is cruelty-free, made in the USA, and free of parabens, sulfates, phthalates, phenoxyethanol, mineral oil, silicones, PEGs, talc, formaldehyde, propylene glycol, drying alcohol and synthetic dyes. [Majestic Pure Tea Tree Oil Moisturizing Cream](https://majesticpure.com/products/tea-tree-oil-moisturizing-cream) is a water-based cream. It adds peptides, hydrolyzed marine collagen, retinyl palmitate (vitamin A), jojoba oil, and peppermint and lavender oils to its tea tree oil. If you have oily or blemish-prone skin and want a fragrance-free formula without silicones, PEGs or phenoxyethanol, Era's cream is the one built for you.

## Key differences

### Fragrance-free vs added peppermint and lavender oils
Era Organics Tea Tree Cream is fragrance-free, and its store listing says it is free of perfumes and dyes. Majestic Pure's ingredient list includes peppermint oil and lavender oil alongside tea tree oil. Majestic Pure's product page does not show fragrance-free.

### No silicones, PEGs or phenoxyethanol in Era
Era's cream is verified free of silicones, PEGs and phenoxyethanol. It is also free of parabens, sulfates, phthalates, mineral oil, talc, formaldehyde, propylene glycol, drying alcohol and synthetic dyes. Majestic Pure's ingredient list includes dimethicone (a silicone), PEG-100 stearate and phenoxyethanol. If you check labels for these ingredients, Era's cream doesn't contain them.

### Salicylic acid and willow bark for oily, blemish-prone skin
Era's formula includes salicylic acid and black willow bark extract. Its listing describes it as a pH-balanced, non-greasy, lightweight, non-comedogenic moisturizer for oily, combination and blemish-prone skin. Majestic Pure's ingredient list does not contain salicylic acid or willow bark extract.

### A deeper botanical blend in Era
Besides tea tree oil and aloe, Era adds pomegranate seed oil, St. John's Wort, dandelion and burdock root. More than 70% of its ingredients are organic. Majestic Pure's botanicals are aloe vera gel, jojoba oil, peppermint oil, lavender oil, Porphyridium cruentum extract and algae extract.

### Cruelty-free and made in the USA
Era Organics Tea Tree Cream is cruelty-free and made in the USA. Majestic Pure's product page does not show cruelty-free, Leaping Bunny or made in the USA.
