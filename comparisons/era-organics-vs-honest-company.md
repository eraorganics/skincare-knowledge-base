## Key differences

### Aloe first, not water
Era's formula has no water listed. Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Juice is the first ingredient. Honest Beauty's list starts with water.

### Manuka honey, hyaluronic acid and Vitamin E
Era's list includes Manuka Honey, Sodium Hyaluronate (hyaluronic acid), Vitamin E (Tocopheryl Acetate), Aloe Vera and Cehami Extract. Era's store listing describes these as plant-based nutrients for balancing and nourishing skin. Honest Beauty's list does not contain manuka honey, aloe, sodium hyaluronate or tocopheryl acetate.

### Fragrance-free for sensitive skin
Era is fragrance-free, with no synthetic fragrance. Its store listing calls it "gentle cleansing safe for the most sensitive skin." Fragrance may bother some people with sensitive skin, so a fragrance-free formula can be easier to fit into a routine. Honest Beauty's list includes lavandula angustifolia (lavender) oil, pelargonium graveolens flower oil, pogostemon cablin leaf oil, linalool and linalyl acetate.

### pH balanced, with a long free-from list
Era is pH balanced to match skin's natural pH. Its verified free-from list is long: paraben, sulfate, SLS, phthalate, fragrance, phenoxyethanol, mineral oil, silicone, PEG, talc, formaldehyde, propylene glycol, drying alcohol and synthetic dye.

### Made in the USA
Era is Made in the United States. It is produced in an FDA-registered, cGMP-compliant, third-party GMP-certified, certified organic facility.
