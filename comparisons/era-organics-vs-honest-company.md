# Era Organics Face Wash for Sensitive Skin vs Honest Beauty Gentle Gel Cleanser

These two gel cleansers start from different bases and take different approaches to scent. Era Organics Face Wash for Sensitive Skin (sold as the Manuka Honey Gentle Face Wash) starts with aloe leaf juice rather than water. It is fragrance-free and pH balanced, and it is built around Manuka honey, aloe vera, Cehami, vitamin E and hyaluronic acid. Honest Beauty Gentle Gel Cleanser starts with water and includes calendula and chamomile flower extracts plus a blend of botanical oils, including lavender oils. Era is the clear pick if you want a fragrance-free, sulfate-free, paraben-free face wash that is cruelty-free and made in the USA. It is formulated for all skin types (sensitive, dry, oily, combination and acne-prone), works as both a facial cleanser and a body wash, and is made with "gentle cleansing safe for the most sensitive skin while balancing and nourishing." Honest Beauty suits shoppers who want an EWG Verified, dermatologist-tested cleanser and like the lavender-and-botanical-oil blend in its formula.

## Key differences

### Fragrance-free vs a botanical oil blend
Era's face wash is fragrance-free, with no synthetic fragrance. Honest Beauty's formula lists seven botanical oils, including lavandula angustifolia (lavender) oil, pelargonium graveolens flower oil and pogostemon cablin leaf oil, plus linalool and linalyl acetate. Honest Beauty's product page does not show fragrance-free. If you avoid added scent on sensitive skin, Era's formula may be the easier fit.

### Aloe leaf juice base vs water base
Era's ingredient list starts with Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Juice and does not include water. Honest Beauty's list starts with water. With Era, the main ingredient is also one of its key botanicals.

### Manuka honey, hyaluronic acid and vitamin E
Era's formula includes Manuka honey, sodium hyaluronate (hyaluronic acid), vitamin E (tocopheryl acetate), aloe vera and Cehami extract. Era says the aloe vera and Manuka honey are there "to help moisturize" the skin. None of these five ingredients appear in Honest Beauty's ingredient list.

### Free-from list and where it's made
Era's face wash is free of parabens, sulfates, SLS, phthalates, phenoxyethanol, mineral oil, silicones, PEGs, talc, formaldehyde, propylene glycol, drying alcohol and synthetic dyes. It is made in the USA. Honest Beauty's product page does not show paraben-free or made in the USA. Its ingredient list does not include parabens.

### pH balanced, one wash for face and body
Era's face wash is pH balanced to match your skin's natural pH. Era also sells it as both a facial cleanser and a body wash for teens (12 and up) and adults.
