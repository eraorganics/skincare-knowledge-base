# Era Organics Diaper Rash Cream vs DESITIN Maximum Strength Original Paste

Both are zinc oxide diaper rash products, and their formulas differ in clear ways. DESITIN Maximum Strength Original Paste contains 40% zinc oxide in a base of petrolatum, cod liver oil and lanolin, and it lists fragrance. Era Organics Diaper Rash Cream contains 10% zinc oxide in a blend of plant oils and butters: plantain, calendula, comfrey, rosehip seed, aloe, jojoba, shea and cocoa butter. It is USDA Organic certified, cruelty-free and made in the USA, and it is fragrance-free. It is free from parabens, phthalates, sulfates, mineral oil, silicones, PEGs, talc, propylene glycol, drying alcohols and synthetic dyes. Era suits parents who want a certified organic, fragrance-free cream with a suggested minimum age of 0. DESITIN suits parents who want a 40% zinc oxide paste with a petrolatum base.

## Key differences

### USDA Organic certified
Era Organics Diaper Rash Cream is USDA Organic certified. DESITIN's product page does not show USDA Organic, certified organic or Ecocert / COSMOS.

### Fragrance-free vs. fragrance listed
Era is fragrance-free. DESITIN lists fragrance on its ingredient list. Parents who want to keep added scent away from a baby's diaper area may prefer a fragrance-free formula.

### Plant oils and butters vs. a petrolatum base
Era's base is plant oils and butters with beeswax: plantain, calendula, comfrey, rosehip seed, aloe, jojoba, shea and cocoa butter. Era is also free from mineral oil and silicones. DESITIN's base starts with petrolatum, cod liver oil and lanolin. Petrolatum does not appear on Era's ingredient list.

### Zinc oxide strength
Era contains 10% zinc oxide. DESITIN contains 40% zinc oxide. The two products are built differently: Era pairs its zinc oxide with a full blend of botanicals, while DESITIN's paste is built around a higher zinc oxide percentage.

### Cruelty-free and made in the USA
Era is cruelty-free and made in the USA. DESITIN's product page does not show cruelty-free, Leaping Bunny or made in the USA.
