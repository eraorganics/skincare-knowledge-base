# Era Organics Organic Nipple Cream vs Earth Mama Organic Nipple Butter

Era Organics Organic Nipple Cream (sold as Organic Nipple Butter) and Earth Mama Organic Nipple Butter are both certified organic balms for breastfeeding moms, and neither lists water as an ingredient. The main differences are in the formulas and labels. Era uses 11 ingredients, including chamomile extract, evening primrose oil, apricot oil and arrowroot powder. Earth Mama uses six ingredients built on olive oil, beeswax and three butters. Era is USDA Organic certified, cruelty-free and made in the USA. Its free-from list is verified across 13 items, including fragrance, parabens, phthalates, mineral oil, silicones and phenoxyethanol. The store lists it as food-grade and safe for breastfeeding. If you want a balm that moisturizes, soothes and comforts sore, chapped skin, with a long verified free-from list behind it, Era is a strong pick.

## Key differences

### Chamomile, evening primrose and apricot oil are only in Era's formula
Era's list includes Chamomile Extract, Evening Primrose Oil, Apricot Oil, Arrowroot Powder, Vegetable Glycerin and Rosemary Extract. None of these appear on Earth Mama's list. Earth Mama's formula includes olive oil, shea butter and mango butter, which Era's list does not. Both share beeswax, cocoa butter and calendula.

### A free-from list verified across 13 items
Era is verified free of parabens, sulfates, phthalates, fragrance, phenoxyethanol, mineral oil, silicones, PEGs, talc, formaldehyde, propylene glycol, drying alcohol and synthetic dyes. Earth Mama's product page states "no lanolin, petroleum, vitamin e, aloe or artificial preservatives." Earth Mama's product page does not show fragrance-free, paraben-free or made in the USA.

### Made in the USA
Era is made in the USA, and the store lists USA as its country of origin.

### Labeled food-grade and safe for breastfeeding
Era's store listing labels it "Food-grade, Safe for Breastfeeding." It is made for breastfeeding mothers, nursing moms and new moms dealing with sore or cracked nipples, chapped skin and breastfeeding irritation.
