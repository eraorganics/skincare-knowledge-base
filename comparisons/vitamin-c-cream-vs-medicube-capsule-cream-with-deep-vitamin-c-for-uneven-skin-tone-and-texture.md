# Era Organics Vitamin C Cream vs medicube Deep Vita C Capsule Cream

The two creams take different routes to vitamin C. Era Organics Vitamin C Cream gets its vitamin C from Kakadu plum, which Era's store listing calls the "highest natural source" of vitamin C. It adds L-ascorbic acid and sets both in a base of aloe leaf juice, cacao seed butter, coconut, jojoba, avocado and carrot seed oils. It is fragrance-free, certified cruelty-free and made in the USA, in an FDA-registered, cGMP-compliant facility. It is also free of parabens, sulfates, phthalates, silicones, PEGs, mineral oil, talc, formaldehyde, propylene glycol, drying alcohols and synthetic dyes. medicube Deep Vita C Capsule Cream, made by APR Co., Ltd., is a water-based cream. Its list includes several forms of vitamin C, niacinamide, alpha-arbutin and adenosine, and it lists fragrance. If you want a short, plant-led vitamin C moisturizer with no added fragrance, Era is the clear pick. If you want a long list of vitamin C forms and vitamins, medicube is built that way.

## Key differences

### Fragrance-free vs. fragrance listed
Era Organics Vitamin C Cream is fragrance-free. medicube's ingredient list includes Fragrance. If you leave added scent out of your routine, Era fits without a second thought.

### Vitamin C from Kakadu plum
Era builds its vitamin C around Kakadu plum, which its listing calls the highest natural source of vitamin C, and adds L-ascorbic acid. medicube lists six vitamin C ingredients: Sodium Ascorbyl Phosphate, Ascorbyl Glucoside, 3-O-Ethyl Ascorbic Acid, Ascorbic Acid, Ascorbyl Propyl Hyaluronate and Ascorbyl Palmitate. Both lists include Sodium Hyaluronate (a form of hyaluronic acid).

### A base made of plant butters and oils
Era starts with aloe leaf juice, not water. It is built on cacao seed butter, coconut oil, jojoba oil, avocado oil and carrot seed oil, plus calendula and green tea (Camellia Sinensis) extracts. Era's listing describes it as a plant-based formula "packed with antioxidants" for brighter, smoother-looking skin. medicube's list starts with Hippophae Rhamnoides Water, Glycerin and Water, and its plant oils are sunflower, soybean and Bixa Orellana seed oils.

### Cruelty-free and made in the USA
Era Organics Vitamin C Cream is certified cruelty-free and made in the USA. medicube's product page does not show cruelty-free, Leaping Bunny or made in the USA.

### A long free-from list
Era's formula is free of parabens, sulfates, phthalates, fragrance, mineral oil, silicones, PEGs, talc, formaldehyde, propylene glycol, drying alcohols and synthetic dyes. Every one of those is confirmed for this cream.

## Which one should you choose?

- **You want a fragrance-free vitamin C cream:** Era Organics Vitamin C Cream. It contains no fragrance.
- **You prefer plant butters and oils:** Era. Kakadu plum, aloe, cacao butter, coconut, jojoba, avocado and carrot seed oil are its key ingredients.
- **Cruelty-free and made in the USA matter to you:** Era holds both.
- **You're working on the look of fine lines, dullness and uneven tone:** Era's listing names wrinkles, fine lines, dullness, hyperpigmentation and premature aging as the concerns it was made for. It suggests use from age 25 and up, on both the face and the eye area.
- **You want many vitamin C forms plus niacinamide and alpha-arbutin in one water-based cream:** medicube lists all of these, and its page says it is dermatologist-tested.
