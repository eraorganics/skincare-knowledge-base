# Era Organics Honey & Oats Manuka Honey Eczema Cream vs Eucerin Eczema Relief Body Cream with Colloidal Oatmeal

Both creams list oatmeal and are labeled steroid-free and fragrance-free. Their formulas differ on most other points. Era Organics Honey & Oats Manuka Honey Eczema Cream is an OTC skin protectant that helps relieve itching and irritation due to eczema. It uses Colloidal Oatmeal 1% along with medical-grade Manuka honey, aloe, shea butter, cocoa butter, MSM, carrot seed oil and hemp seed oil. It is Cruelty-Free, Made in the United States, clinically tested and dermatologically tested. It is free from parabens, sulfates, phthalates, fragrance, mineral oil, petrolatum, silicones, PEGs, talc, formaldehyde, propylene glycol, drying alcohols, synthetic dyes, steroids and hydrocortisone. Its listing says it suits all skin types, including sensitive, dry, irritated and eczema-prone skin, and is safe for daily use on kids and adults. Eucerin Eczema Relief Body Cream with Colloidal Oatmeal, from Beiersdorf Inc., lists Oatmeal, Ceramide 3, Mineral Oil and Glycyrrhiza Inflata Root. ## Key differences

### Manuka honey and a wide mix of butters and seed oils
Era's formula includes medical-grade Manuka honey, aloe, shea butter, cocoa butter, carrot seed oil, hemp seed oil, olive oil, sunflower seed oil and oat kernel oil. None of these is on Eucerin's ingredient list.

### No mineral oil and no PEGs
Era is free from mineral oil, petrolatum and PEGs. Eucerin's ingredient list includes Mineral Oil and Peg-40 Stearate.

### Extra ingredients alongside the oatmeal
Era adds Hyaluronic Acid, Sodium PCA, DL-Panthenol (Vitamin B5), L-Ascorbic Acid, MSM and Zinc Oxide to its Colloidal Oatmeal 1%. Eucerin pairs Oatmeal with Ceramide 3 and Glycyrrhiza Inflata Root.

### Cruelty-free and made in the USA
Era is Cruelty-Free and Made in the United States. Eucerin's product page does not show cruelty-free, Leaping Bunny or made in the USA.
