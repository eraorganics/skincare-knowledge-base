# Era Organics Honey & Oats Manuka Honey Eczema Cream vs Eucerin Eczema Relief Body Cream with Colloidal Oatmeal

Both creams use oats, and both are steroid-free, fragrance-free and paraben-free. Past that, the formulas are quite different. Era Organics Honey & Oats Manuka Honey Eczema Cream is an OTC skin protectant that helps relieve itching and irritation due to eczema. Its active ingredient is 1% colloidal oatmeal. Around it sits a long list of named ingredients: medical-grade Manuka honey, aloe, shea butter, cocoa butter, MSM, carrot seed oil, hemp seed oil, hyaluronic acid and vitamin B5. Era is cruelty-free and made in the USA. It is clinically and dermatologically tested, and it is free of mineral oil, PEGs, silicones, sulfates, phthalates, synthetic dyes, steroids and hydrocortisone. Eucerin Eczema Relief Body Cream is made by Beiersdorf, Inc. Its label lists Oatmeal, Ceramide 3, Mineral Oil and Glycyrrhiza Inflata Root, and its product page says the cream is accepted by the National Eczema Association. If you want a fragrance-free oat cream with Manuka honey and a range of butters and seed oils, with no mineral oil and no PEGs, Era is the one built for that.

## Key differences

### Manuka honey and a longer list of named extras
Era pairs its colloidal oatmeal with Manuka honey, aloe, shea butter, cocoa butter, MSM, carrot seed oil, hemp seed oil, hyaluronic acid and vitamin B5 (DL-Panthenol). The store listing calls it a "16-in-1 formula." None of these ingredients is on Eucerin's list. Eucerin's list includes Ceramide 3, which is not on Era's.

### No mineral oil, no PEGs
Era is mineral-oil-free and PEG-free. Eucerin's list includes Mineral Oil and Peg-40 Stearate. If you avoid either of these, Era's label already rules them out. Era is also free of silicones, sulfates, phthalates, talc, formaldehyde, propylene glycol, drying alcohol and synthetic dyes.

### Cruelty-free and made in the USA
Era is cruelty-free and made in the USA. Eucerin's product page does not show cruelty-free, Leaping Bunny or made in the USA.

### Tested on skin, for every age
Era's store listing says it is clinically tested and dermatologically tested. The listing also says it is safe for daily use on kids and adults, with a suggested minimum age of 0. Eucerin's product page describes Eucerin as "a dermatologist and pediatrician-recommended brand."
