# Era Organics Hypochlorous Acid Spray vs Magic Molecule Daily Skin Spray

If you want a fragrance-free, cruelty-free daily mist that cleanses, soothes and comforts skin from head to toe, and that the whole family can use from birth, Era is the clear pick. ## Key differences

### A three-ingredient formula with a stated concentration
Era's spray has just three ingredients: Aqua, Sodium Chloride and Hypochlorous Acid at .02%. Magic Molecule lists four: ionized water, sodium chloride, hypochlorous acid and hypochlorite ion. If you like knowing exactly what goes on your skin and at what strength, Era's label spells it out.

### A verified free-from list
Era Organics Hypochlorous Acid Spray is verified free of parabens, sulfates, phthalates, fragrance, phenoxyethanol, mineral oil, silicones, PEGs, talc, formaldehyde, propylene glycol, drying alcohol and synthetic dyes. Era also describes it as sting-free and alcohol-free. Magic Molecule's product page does not show fragrance-free or paraben-free.

### Cruelty-Free and made in the USA
Era's spray is Cruelty-Free. It is made in the USA in an FDA-registered, cGMP-compliant, third-party GMP-certified facility that is also a certified organic facility. Magic Molecule's product page does not show cruelty-free or made in the USA.

### Patented Next-Gen Single Cell Technology
Era makes its spray with its Patented Next-Gen Single Cell Technology. It comes in a BPA-free, recyclable spray bottle.

### Made for the whole family
Era lists its spray as safe for all ages, from babies to adults, with a suggested minimum age of 0. Magic Molecule's page describes its spray as "safe for all ages (1 month+) and skin types."
