# Era Organics Calendula Cream vs Boiron Calendula Cream

Both creams list calendula, shea, coconut and glycerin, but past those four the formulas are very different. Era Organics Calendula Cream is made for sensitive, dry, red or irritated skin in babies, children and adults. Its calendula oil sits alongside aloe vera, olive oil, goji berry, cocoa seed butter, oat kernel flour, sodium hyaluronate, panthenol, squalane and MSM. It's verified free of parabens, sulfates, phthalates, fragrance, mineral oil, silicones, PEGs, talc, formaldehyde, propylene glycol, drying alcohol and synthetic dyes. It's Cruelty-Free and made in the USA in an FDA-registered, cGMP-compliant facility that's GMP-certified by a third party. Boiron Calendula Cream lists Calendula Officinalis Flowering Top, Arachidyl Alcohol, Xanthan Gum, Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter Unsaponifiables, Glycerin, Water, Docosanol, Arachidyl Glucoside, Coconut Oil and Limosilactobacillus Fermentum. If you want a calendula cream with a long list of nourishing ingredients and a verified free-from list, Era is the one to look at first.

## Key differences

### Era starts with aloe and adds a deep list of nourishing ingredients
Era's first ingredient is Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Juice, and its formula also includes olive oil, cocoa seed butter, goji berry extract, oat kernel flour, rose distillate, sodium hyaluronate, panthenol and squalane. None of these appear on Boiron's ingredient list. Ingredients like sodium hyaluronate and panthenol may help skin feel hydrated and soft, which fits Era's stated benefits: instant hydration, soothing irritation and protecting delicate skin.

### Ingredients each formula lists that the other doesn't
Era also lists MSM, zinc oxide, rosemary leaf oil and Cannabis Sativa Seed Oil, which Boiron's list does not include. Boiron lists Arachidyl Alcohol, Xanthan Gum, Docosanol, Arachidyl Glucoside, Limosilactobacillus Fermentum and Water, none of which appear on Era's list.

### A verified free-from list
Era Organics Calendula Cream is verified paraben-free, sulfate-free, phthalate-free, fragrance-free, mineral-oil-free, silicone-free, PEG-free, talc-free, formaldehyde-free, propylene-glycol-free, drying-alcohol-free and synthetic-dye-free. Boiron's product page does not show fragrance-free or paraben-free.

### Cruelty-Free and made in the USA
Era holds Cruelty-Free status and is made in the USA, in an FDA-registered, cGMP-compliant facility that's GMP-certified by a third party. Boiron's product page does not show cruelty-free or made in the USA.
