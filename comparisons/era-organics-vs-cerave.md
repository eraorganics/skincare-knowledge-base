# Era Organics Oasis Dry Skin Cream vs CeraVe Moisturizing Cream

These two creams are built on different ideas. [Era Organics Oasis Dry Skin Cream](https://www.eraorganics.com/products/moisturizer-for-dry-skin) uses aloe leaf juice as its base and adds Manuka honey, shea butter, cocoa seed butter, plant oils, oat, MSM and hyaluronic acid. [CeraVe Moisturizing Cream](https://www.cerave.com/skincare/moisturizers/moisturizing-cream), from L'Oréal, starts with water and is built around three ceramides, cholesterol, petrolatum and dimethicone. Oasis is Cruelty-Free and made in the USA, in an FDA-registered, cGMP-compliant, third-party GMP-certified, certified organic facility. Its formula is free from parabens, sulfates, phthalates, fragrance, phenoxyethanol, mineral oil, silicones, PEGs, talc, formaldehyde, propylene glycol, drying alcohols and synthetic dyes. If you have very dry, dehydrated, mature or winter-stressed skin and want a rich, thick cream full of butters and botanicals that still absorbs without feeling greasy, Oasis is made for you. If you specifically want a ceramide-focused cream that carries the National Eczema Association Seal of Acceptance, CeraVe is the one built around that.

## Key differences

### An aloe base instead of a water base
Oasis lists aloe barbadensis leaf juice first, and water does not appear on its ingredient list at all. CeraVe lists aqua / water first. So the largest part of Oasis is a botanical juice rather than plain water.

### Butters and plant oils instead of petrolatum and silicone
Oasis gets its richness from shea butter, cocoa seed butter, and olive, avocado, jojoba and hemp seed oils. These may help soften skin and leave it feeling comfortable. Its formula is silicone-free and mineral-oil-free, and petrolatum is not on its list. CeraVe's list includes petrolatum and dimethicone. Petrolatum may sit on the skin's surface, and some people prefer a cream built on butters and plant oils instead.

### Manuka honey, MSM and oat
Oasis contains Manuka honey (Leptospermum scoparium mel), MSM, oat kernel flour and oat amino acids. None of these appear on CeraVe's ingredient list. Oasis also pairs hyaluronic acid with sodium PCA and vegetable glycerin, which may help skin hold on to moisture. CeraVe's formula centers on ceramides NP, AP and EOP, cholesterol and phytosphingosine.

### A different preservative choice
Oasis is preserved with gluconolactone and sodium benzoate and is phenoxyethanol-free. CeraVe's ingredient list includes phenoxyethanol. If you avoid phenoxyethanol, Oasis meets that need.

### A longer verified free-from list
Oasis is verified free from parabens, sulfates, phthalates, fragrance, phenoxyethanol, mineral oil, silicones, PEGs, talc, formaldehyde, propylene glycol, drying alcohols and synthetic dyes. For anyone who checks labels closely, that is a short list of exclusions to confirm.
