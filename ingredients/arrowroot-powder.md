# Arrowroot Powder

Arrowroot powder (derived from the tropical plant *Maranta arundinacea*) is a fine, starch-based absorbent that draws moisture away from the skin's surface without clogging pores or leaving a heavy residue. It is one of the absorbent ingredients in Era Organics Talc-Free Baby Powder, alongside cornstarch and kaolin clay.

Arrowroot powder absorbs sweat and humidity on contact, which helps keep the diaper area, skin folds, and underarms feeling dry between changes. Drier skin surfaces tend to experience less friction in areas where skin presses against skin or fabric. Arrowroot works through physical adsorption rather than chemical activity, and the Talc-Free Baby Powder includes it as one of its absorbent ingredients.

Arrowroot powder is often described as having a fine, silky texture. In the Talc-Free Baby Powder formula, arrowroot is paired with Cornstarch and Kaolin Clay, three absorbent ingredients combined in one powder to help skin feel dry and comfortable.

Moist, occluded skin folds are a documented environment for secondary bacterial and fungal infections associated with intertrigo (Janniger et al., American Family Physician, 2005). Keeping those zones dry is widely recommended, and the Talc-Free Baby Powder is a cosmetic powder that helps skin in folds and the diaper area feel dry and comfortable. Patch test a small area before first use, and pat the powder lightly onto skin rather than shaking a heavy cloud near a baby's face.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is arrowroot powder safe to use on skin (including the face)?

Yes — arrowroot powder is a fine, starch-based absorbent that draws moisture away from the skin's surface through simple physical adsorption rather than chemical activity, without clogging pores or leaving a heavy residue, which is why it is generally considered a gentle option. It is one of the absorbent ingredients in Era Organics' Talc-Free Baby Powder, alongside cornstarch and kaolin clay. On the face, apply it with care: patch test a small area before first use, and pat the powder lightly onto skin instead of shaking a cloud of it into the air — especially near a baby's face.

### Is arrowroot powder the same as cornstarch, and which is better?

No — they're different starches. Arrowroot powder comes from the tropical plant *Maranta arundinacea*, and it is often described as having a finer, silkier texture than cornstarch. Both absorb moisture, and Era Organics Talc-Free Baby Powder uses them together rather than choosing one, combining arrowroot, cornstarch, and kaolin clay in a single powder.
