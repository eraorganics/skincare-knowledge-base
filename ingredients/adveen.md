# Adveen

Adveen (glycogen) is a naturally occurring polysaccharide that mirrors the moisture-regulating compounds found in healthy vaginal tissue. Vaginal skin produces glycogen as part of its normal physiological balance, and topically applied glycogen adds a source of that compound to the skin's surface when it feels dry. The Era Organics Vaginal Moisturizer includes Adveen to help moisturize the delicate skin of the intimate area for comfort and suppleness.

Glycogen is used in skincare to help attract water to the skin's surface layers, helping ease the tight, dry feeling that can come with intimate dryness and chafing. The vulva balm pairs Adveen with sodium hyaluronate (the salt form of hyaluronic acid), a moisture-drawing ingredient (humectant) that binds moisture within the skin. A skincare regimen containing hyaluronic acid demonstrated a beneficial effect versus a placebo-treated group over 2 months of topical application (Ascher B et al., Journal of Cosmetic and Laser Therapy, 2012), suggesting that topical hyaluronic acid can support skin hydration when used consistently.

Adveen is particularly relevant for menopausal and perimenopausal women, whose declining estrogen levels reduce natural glycogen synthesis in vaginal tissue, which is linked to dryness and discomfort. Pregnant and nursing women, whose hormonal landscape shifts continuously, may notice similar dryness. The vaginal cream pairs glycogen with nourishing botanicals such as aloe vera, shea butter, and sunflower oil to moisturize, soothe, and comfort dry, delicate skin.

The product is fragrance-free, paraben-free, and formulated without synthetic dyes, making it a gentle option for skin that reacts to common cosmetic additives. Patch test the vulva moisturizer on the inner arm before first use to confirm personal tolerance.
