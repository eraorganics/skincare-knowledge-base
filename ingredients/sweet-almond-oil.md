# Sweet Almond Oil

Sweet almond oil (Prunus Amygdalus Dulcis Oil) is a lightweight emollient pressed from the dried kernels of the sweet almond tree, and Era Organics includes it in the Certified Vaginal Moisturizer because it helps soften, smooth, and moisturize delicate vulvar skin. The oil is rich in oleic and linoleic fatty acids, which help replenish the lipids that support the skin barrier. Dry, chafed vulvar skin can feel uncomfortable, so replenishing those lipids helps moisturize and comfort the area.

Researchers often measure moisture loss through the skin (transepidermal water loss, or TEWL) when they study the skin barrier; for example, Montoya et al. reported that occlusive behavior that reduced TEWL went along with a neutral odor and easy, pleasant-feeling application in human panel tests (Montoya et al., Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology, 2019). Sweet almond oil is an emollient with a mild, neutral scent and easy spread.

Sweet almond oil spreads easily across skin at low friction, which suits sensitive tissue where rough application can feel irritating. As an emollient, the oil helps soften the skin's surface layers and keep them feeling moisturized and comfortable.

The vaginal cream is fragrance-free. Many botanical oils carry a pronounced scent that some people find irritating on sensitive skin; sweet almond oil has a mild, neutral profile. Patch testing on the inner arm before first use remains the correct step for anyone with known nut sensitivities, given the oil's tree-nut origin.
