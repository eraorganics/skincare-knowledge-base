# Carrot Seed Oil

Carrot seed oil (Daucus carota sativa) is a botanical oil pressed from the seeds of the wild carrot plant, included in the Honey & Oats Manuka Honey Eczema Cream for its skin-nourishing and soothing properties. The oil is rich in beta-carotene and tocopherols, which are antioxidant compounds that help protect the skin surface from oxidative stress — a factor that worsens dry, reactive, and rash-prone skin.

Carrot seed oil softens the outer skin layer and helps maintain suppleness in skin affected by chronic dryness. For eczema-prone or dermatitis-prone skin, where the barrier is already disrupted, an emollient that buffers environmental stress without adding fragrance or synthetic dyes is a practical fit. Era Organics Honey & Oats Manuka Honey Eczema Cream is fragrance-free and synthetic-dye-free, so carrot seed oil contributes its soothing character without common irritant carriers.

The oil works alongside other moisturizing ingredients in the Eczema Honey & Oats cream to comfort dry, eczema-prone skin. Atopic dermatitis is a prevalent inflammatory skin condition affecting both children and adults, with a global prevalence of 15–30% (Chaudhary et al., Journal of Biotechnology and Biomedicine, 2024). Within a formula made for eczema-prone skin, each ingredient carries a defined role: carrot seed oil moisturizes and softens while the formula's colloidal oatmeal 1% serves as the skin protectant active.

Era Organics Honey & Oats Manuka Honey Eczema Cream is formulated without fragrance, synthetic dyes, or steroids, so the choice to include carrot seed oil reflects a deliberate formulation approach. Patch testing before first use is the recommended practice, particularly for users with known sensitivities to Asteraceae-family botanicals, since carrot (Daucus carota) belongs to the Apiaceae family and is botanically distinct, but individual sensitivities vary and a patch test on a small area of skin before applying the Honey & Oats Manuka Honey Eczema Cream broadly is sound practice.
