# Hemp Seed Oil

Hemp seed oil (Cannabis Sativa seed oil) is a plant-derived oil pressed from hemp seeds that delivers linoleic acid and gamma-linolenic acid directly to the skin barrier. Skin affected by dermatitis and eczema typically shows a disrupted lipid barrier, and these fatty acids can help replenish the lipid layer. Hemp seed oil's fatty acid profile mirrors the skin's own intercellular lipids, so the oil absorbs without leaving a heavy residue.

Era Organics Honey & Oats Manuka Honey Eczema Cream includes hemp seed oil specifically for its fatty acid composition. Linoleic acid is the predominant fatty acid in healthy outer skin layer (stratum corneum) lipids, and barrier-compromised skin loses it at an accelerated rate. Hemp seed oil reintroduces linoleic acid topically. A systematic review and meta-analysis of complementary and alternative medicine treatments found a standard mean difference of -0.70 (95% CI -1.19, -0.21) in the eczema area and severity index score, supporting the relevance of plant-derived ingredients in eczema management (Jones et al., JAAD International, 2021).

The Honey & Oats Manuka Honey Eczema Cream pairs hemp seed oil with colloidal oatmeal 1%, the active skin protectant. Colloidal oatmeal forms a protective film over inflamed skin. Era Organics Honey & Oats Manuka Honey Eczema Cream rounds out this pairing with glycerin, shea butter, and hyaluronic acid.

Patch test before first use, particularly on skin with a known sensitivity to Cannabis Sativa botanicals.
