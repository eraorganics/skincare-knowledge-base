## The protocol

### Morning routine

1. Cleanse with Face Wash Sensitive
2. Vitamin C Cream — antioxidant protection + collagen cofactor for daytime
3. Methylene Blue Drops — mitochondrial support + additional antioxidant activity
4. Eye Cream Balm — periorbital protection
5. Sunscreen SPF 30+ (UV causes 80% of visible aging — non-negotiable)

### Evening routine

1. Cleanse with Face Wash Sensitive
2. Anti-Wrinkle Serum — peptide delivery for overnight collagen synthesis (production peaks during sleep)
3. Eye Cream Balm — overnight periorbital repair
4. Face Moisturizer — barrier support during overnight recovery period

### Weekly treatment

1. Glycolic Acid Peel — 1-2x per week on non-consecutive days
2. Skip Vitamin C and peptide serum on peel nights (acids reduce peptide efficacy)
3. Apply Face Moisturizer after peel as barrier support
