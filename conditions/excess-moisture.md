# Excess moisture

Arrowroot powder, cornstarch, and kaolin clay are the three moisture-absorbing agents at the center of Era Organics Talc-Free Baby Powder's formula. Each of these ingredients is known for absorbing surface moisture, which is why they are common in dusting powders for the diaper area, skin folds, and underarms. Talc free baby powder for excess moisture is built around these ingredients because starches and clays are absorbent materials that take up water, giving the skin a dry, comfortable feel.

Kaolin clay is a naturally occurring phyllosilicate mineral known for absorbing oil and moisture. Its fine particle structure spreads evenly across skin folds and the diaper area, leaving a soft, smooth finish. Arrowroot powder, derived from the tropical Maranta arundinacea plant, is an absorbent starch that leaves a silky, non-gritty texture on the skin, making the Baby Dusting Powder comfortable for repeated daily use.

Cornstarch rounds out the trio as a plant-derived polysaccharide valued in powders for its absorbent, silky texture. Together, the three ingredients give the formula a soft, dry-feeling finish on the diaper area and underarm skin; skin moisture management is an active area of research, with studies examining how starch-based materials interact with water, for example, Adamu et al. Found that extracted starch had a moisture level of 8.00 % and comprised 24.95 % amylose and 75.05 % amylopectin, characteristics that bear on how plant-derived powders handle surface humidity (Adamu et al., International journal of biological macromolecules, 2025). Calendula Extract and Rosemary Extract are paired into the formula to soothe and comfort skin, while Mixed Tocopherols protect the formula's plant-derived ingredients from oxidative breakdown.

The Natural Baby Powder is fragrance-free, paraben-free, and talc-free, so none of those three ingredients are in the formula. Pat a light dusting onto the diaper area or any skin fold prone to moisture buildup, and avoid shaking heavy powder clouds near the face. Patch test a small area before first use, particularly for users with known sensitivity to Asteraceae-family botanicals, as the formula contains Calendula Extract, a plant in that family.
