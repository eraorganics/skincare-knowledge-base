# Rosacea

Hypochlorous acid spray for rosacea-prone skin is a gentle way to soothe and comfort reactive skin with hypochlorous acid (HOCl), a reactive oxygen species and one of the skin's own innate antimicrobial molecules. At a 0.02% concentration, the HOCl in this formula is a light mist that soothes and comforts redness-prone skin and helps protect the skin barrier. White blood cells produce hypochlorous acid naturally during the immune response, and the ingredient in the spray is that same molecule.

The connection between microbial imbalance and rosacea is well established in dermatological literature. Demodex mites and the bacteria associated with them generate surface-level inflammation that worsens redness, flushing, and papule formation. HOCl as an ingredient has been studied for its ability to disrupt those microbial communities on contact. The face mist's three-ingredient formula, Aqua, Sodium Chloride, Hypochlorous Acid, keeps the ingredient list short for sensitized rosacea-prone skin, and it is fragrance-free, free of drying alcohols, and free of synthetic dyes.

Clinical dermatological review describes HOCl's broad use across inflammatory skin conditions. Haralović et al., writing in *Biomedicines* (2025), documented a growing body of clinical studies affirming HOCl's therapeutic role across dermatology, surgery, and ophthalmology; the majority of clinical studies confirmed that HOCl is useful in therapy, with findings showing HOCl's dual capacity to limit microbial activity and modulate inflammatory response (Haralović et al., Biomedicines, 2025). Those two properties of the ingredient are why HOCl draws interest for rosacea-prone skin. Applied as a face toner after cleansing and allowed to dry before layering creams or balms, the HOCL spray fits into a rosacea-prone skincare routine with only three ingredients.
