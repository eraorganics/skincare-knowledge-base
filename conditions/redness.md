Chamomile is a botanical studied for its calming effect on the look of redness in irritated skin. Its active compounds, specifically apigenin and bisabolol, have been studied for calming the vascular response that produces flushing and reactive redness at the skin surface. Calendula Oil, a second botanical in the formula, has been studied for complementary calming action through flavonoids linked to lower inflammatory signaling.
