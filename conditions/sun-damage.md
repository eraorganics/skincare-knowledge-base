# Sun damage

Hypochlorous acid is a reactive oxygen species (an oxidant molecule) that white blood cells produce to neutralize pathogens and reduce inflammatory signaling at wound sites. Sun exposure can leave skin feeling hot, tight, and red, and a light mist of 0.02% hypochlorous acid on those areas helps soothe and comfort the skin's surface. The Era Organics HOCL formula lists just three ingredients: water, sodium chloride, and hypochlorous acid at 0.02%.

Sunburned and windburned skin shares the same surface-level discomfort, and the mist helps soothe and comfort skin that looks red and feels irritated. The skin spray delivers this active at a 0.02% concentration in a fragrance-free formula made for soothing the skin's surface.

Supporting this anti-inflammatory profile, Hon et al., found that a four-week, twice-weekly regimen of diluted sodium hypochlorite (bleach) baths reduced topical corticosteroid use by a mean difference of 1.1 ± 2.6 days/week (p = 0) among all 40 patients who completed the trial; sodium hypochlorite is the oxidized chlorine compound most closely related to hypochlorous acid in the bleach-bath literature (Hon et al., The Journal of Dermatological Treatment, 2016).
