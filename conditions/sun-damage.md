# Sun damage

Hypochlorous acid is an oxidant molecule (a reactive oxygen species) that white blood cells make naturally as part of the body's immune response. Sun exposure can leave skin feeling hot, tight, and red, and a light mist of 0.02% hypochlorous acid on those areas helps soothe and comfort the skin's surface.
