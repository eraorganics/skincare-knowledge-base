# Underarm moisture

Talc free baby powder for underarm moisture is built on three absorbent minerals and starches, Kaolin Clay, Arrowroot Powder, and Cornstarch, ingredients known for absorbing surface moisture and leaving a dry feel on the skin. Kaolin Clay (a fine-particle aluminum silicate) is known for drawing oil and water from the skin's surface and holding them within its layered mineral structure. Arrowroot Powder and Cornstarch are starch-based powders that leave a smooth, dry feel on the skin.

Underarm skin faces a specific challenge: sweat glands in the axillary region are denser than in most body areas, and moisture trapped against that skin softens the outer skin layer (stratum corneum) (the outermost skin layer), raising friction. Starch-based powders demonstrate meaningful moisture-binding capacity; for reference, extracted starch has been shown to carry a moisture level of 8.00%, comprising 24.95% amylose and 75.05% amylopectin, illustrating the structural properties that underpin surface moisture absorption (Adamu et al., International Journal of Biological Macromolecules, 2025). The Talc-Free Baby Powder is a dusting powder that sits on the skin's surface, which helps the underarm area feel drier and more comfortable. Rosemary Extract is included in the formula and contains naturally occurring phenolic compounds with antioxidant character.

Calendula Extract rounds out the formula; calendula, from the Calendula officinalis flower (in the Asteraceae family), has been studied for its soothing properties. Calendula-sensitive individuals, particularly those with ragweed reactions, should patch test the Talc-Free Baby Powder on a small area before broader underarm application. For adults using the Talc-Free Baby Powder daily, pat a light dusting onto dry underarm skin and allow it to settle before dressing; avoid shaking heavy powder clouds near the face.
