### How do you get rid of clogged pores?

Clogged pores happen when debris settles into pores and stays lodged there, and a straightforward at-home approach is a gentle physical exfoliation routine done the right way. Massaging a scrub over the skin in small circular motions helps loosen blackheads and clogged pores, dislodging the debris lodged inside them. The circular technique itself is what does the loosening, so there is no need to press hard — a light, consistent touch works better than aggressive scrubbing.
