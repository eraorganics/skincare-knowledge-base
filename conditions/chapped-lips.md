# Chapped lips

Two kinds of ingredients come up most often for chapped lips: occlusives, which help seal moisture in, and emollients, which soften dry, cracked skin. Era Organics Superbalm contains both. Beeswax is an occlusive that forms a protective film on the skin surface, which slows moisture loss through the skin (transepidermal water loss) (TEWL). Cocoa Butter and Coconut Oil are emollients that supply fatty acids known to soften the outer keratin layer of skin that has become tight or fissured.

Superbalm also contains Calendula Oil and Chamomile, two botanicals that have long been used in balms for sensitive skin. Avocado Oil contributes oleic acid, which integrates readily into the outer skin layer (stratum corneum) (the outermost skin layer). Vegetable Glycerin is a humectant, meaning it draws water toward the skin surface, so it pairs naturally with an occlusive like Beeswax.

Sunflower seed oil applied to compromised skin showed a measurable effect on barrier function in a controlled study comparing natural oils; the same research found that not all natural oils equally support barrier recovery, showing the relevance of oil selection for fragile skin (Danby et al., Pediatric Dermatology, 2013). Sunflower Oil is rich in linoleic acid, a fatty acid found in the skin's own barrier lipids, which sets it apart from heavier saturated oils. Superbalm's formula also includes Arrowroot Powder, an ingredient commonly used in balms to reduce tackiness.

Follow the directions on the Superbalm label. Chamomile is a member of the Asteraceae (ragweed) botanical family; users with ragweed sensitivity should patch test the balm on the inside of the elbow before applying it to the lip area.

### What causes chapped lips?

Chapped lips develop when the lip surface loses moisture faster than the tissue can replenish it — a process called transepidermal water loss (TEWL). As that moisture escapes, the outer keratin layer loses lipid content, tightens, and eventually fissures. In more severe cases, where the barrier has broken down enough to produce redness and rawness, the underlying tissue becomes exposed and the skin struggles to recover on its own.

Caring for chapped lips usually means two things at once: sealing the surface to slow further moisture loss, and replenishing the lipids the keratin layer has lost. Era Organics Superbalm contains ingredients from both groups: Beeswax, an occlusive that forms a protective film, plus Cocoa Butter, Coconut Oil, Avocado Oil, and Sunflower Oil, emollients that supply fatty acids. It also contains Vegetable Glycerin, a humectant, and the botanicals Calendula Oil and Chamomile.
