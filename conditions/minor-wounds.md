## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you treat and care for minor wounds and cuts?

Minor wounds, small cuts, and scrapes do best in a clean, protected environment where the skin surface stays stable. The first step is to gently rinse the area with clean water, then cover it to shield it from further irritation.

Era Organics Superbalm is built on a base of Beeswax and Coconut Oil and is labeled to temporarily protect and help relieve skin affected by eczema, dermatitis, rashes, itch, or irritation, not for wound care. It's formulated without synthetic fragrances, synthetic dyes, parabens, or sulfates.

For a cut or scrape, follow your healthcare provider's guidance on what to apply. A light bandage over the top helps keep the area covered. If a wound is deep, won't stop bleeding, or shows signs of infection — spreading redness, warmth, or discharge — see a healthcare provider.
