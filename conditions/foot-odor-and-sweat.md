# Foot odor and sweat

Era Organics Talc-Free Baby Powder is made with three absorbent ingredients, Arrowroot Powder, Cornstarch, and Kaolin Clay, for a dry, comfortable feel on the skin of your feet. Arrowroot Powder is a fine starch derived from the tropical *Maranta arundinacea* plant, prized for its ability to absorb moisture without caking or leaving residue on skin.

Kaolin Clay is a naturally occurring aluminum silicate mineral with a high surface-area-to-weight ratio, which is why it is widely used as an absorbent in cosmetic powders. Cornstarch is another absorbent starch, known for pulling water molecules into its granular matrix. Together, these three ingredients give the powder its soft, dry feel between the toes and along the arch.

Foot odor originates when bacteria metabolize the amino acids and lipids found in sweat, releasing volatile sulfur compounds and short-chain fatty acids. Research confirms that permeation through skin proceeds via a diffusion-controlled process similar to permeation through an aqueous mucous layer, meaning substances applied at the surface interact with skin through a comparable diffusion mechanism (Konrádsdóttir et al., The Journal of pharmacy and pharmacology, 2009). Keeping feet clean and dry is a common part of everyday foot care. Era Organics Talc-Free Baby Powder is fragrance-free, so it adds no synthetic scent compounds, which commonly irritate sensitive areas.
