Chamomile, one of Superbalm's key ingredients, contains bisabolol and apigenin, compounds that have been studied for calming the skin. A randomized controlled trial found that chamomile liposomal gel reduced the rate of dry natural skin shedding (desquamation) in participants with treatment-related dermatitis to 76.9% compared to 88.9% in the control group receiving gel without chamomile (Menêses et al., 2024). A pilot randomized controlled trial assigning 115 neonates to sunflower oil, olive oil, or no oil twice daily for four weeks measured trans-epidermal water loss, outer skin layer (stratum corneum) hydration, and lipid lamellae spectral profiles as outcomes (Cooke et al., Acta Dermato-Venereologica, 2016). Sunflower oil's fatty acid profile has been studied for its role in the structural lipids of the outer skin layer (stratum corneum).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is eczema different from dermatitis and acne, and what's mistaken for it?

Eczema and dermatitis are often confused, but eczema IS dermatitis — specifically atopic dermatitis, a chronic inflammatory condition driven by two core problems: a compromised skin barrier that loses water rapidly through depleted lipid layers, and an overactive inflammatory response at the skin surface.

"Dermatitis" is the broader term covering any skin inflammation, including contact dermatitis triggered by a specific external irritant. Contact dermatitis can look nearly identical to eczema — same redness, same itch — but it clears when the trigger is removed. Fragrance, parabens, and synthetic dyes are among the most common contact triggers, so a formula that's fragrance-free, paraben-free, and free from synthetic dyes leaves those ingredients out from the start.

Beeswax and cocoa butter then add an occlusive layer that helps slow moisture escape.
