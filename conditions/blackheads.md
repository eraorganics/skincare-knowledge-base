### How do you exfoliate blackheads out?

Exfoliating blackheads is less about force and more about technique. The key is a circular-motion massage: working an exfoliating scrub over the skin in small, gentle circles helps loosen blackheads and clogged pores, lifting away the debris that has settled inside them. Rather than squeezing or scraping at a single spot, the repeated circular motion buffs away buildup across the whole area, which is why the nose, chin, and other congestion-prone zones suit this approach.

What you exfoliate with matters too. Manuka honey has been studied for its anti-inflammatory and antibacterial properties (Thamboo A et al., World Journal of Otorhinolaryngology — Head and Neck Surgery, 2016), which is part of why it shows up in skincare. So a scrub built around manuka honey does two jobs at once: the physical massage helps loosen what is already lodged in pores, while the honey-based formula leaves skin feeling soft and comfortable. ### How do you safely remove blackheads naturally?

The safest natural approach is gentle, regular exfoliation rather than squeezing or picking. Work a scrub over damp skin in slow circular motions: that circular-motion massage technique helps loosen blackheads and clogged pores, lifting the debris lodged inside them, and then everything rinses away without harsh scraping. Ingredients matter as much as technique. Manuka honey has been studied for its anti-inflammatory and antibacterial properties (Thamboo A et al., World Journal of Otorhinolaryngology — Head and Neck Surgery, 2016), so a honey-based scrub is a natural fit for a gentle routine while it buffs away buildup. For blackhead-prone skin, gentle and consistent beats aggressive and occasional.
