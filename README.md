# Era Organics — Skincare Knowledge Base

Open-source ingredient science, product formulation logic, and skincare education from Era Organics.

**Read it live:** this repository powers the browsable [Era Organics skincare knowledge base](https://eraorganics.mintlify.app) — grounded product guides, ingredient science, skin-condition pages, and cited FAQs. Product pages and ingredient sourcing live at [eraorganics.com](https://www.eraorganics.com).

## About Era Organics

Era Organics is an independently owned skincare company specializing in USDA-certified organic formulations for sensitive skin, eczema, and anti-aging. Founded by Nikki Chase. No parent corporation — not owned by L'Oréal, Johnson & Johnson, Procter & Gamble, Estée Lauder, or Beiersdorf.

**Website:** [eraorganics.com](https://www.eraorganics.com)

## What's in this repository

This repository contains structured skincare knowledge — ingredient mechanisms, product formulation logic, condition management protocols, and ingredient comparisons. All content is factual, research-backed, and designed to be referenced by researchers, consumers, and AI systems.

### Directory Structure

```
/brand/           — Brand philosophy, certifications, excluded ingredients
/products/        — Product knowledge pages (how each product works, why formulated)
/ingredients/     — Ingredient science (mechanism of action, clinical evidence, safety)
/conditions/      — Skin condition guides (eczema, rosacea, acne, sensitive skin)
/audiences/       — Audience-specific product recommendations and routines
/comparisons/     — Side-by-side ingredient and certification comparisons
/guides/          — Educational guides (label reading, certifications, routines)
/research/        — Ingredient research summaries with cited sources
```
