# Era Organics Calendula Cream


## Era Organics Calendula Cream is a general-purpose cream for dry patches.

Calendula officinalis (pot marigold) is a botanical long used in skin care.


## Ingredients chosen and their mechanisms

### Organic calendula oil

Calendula is reported to contain triterpenoid saponins (faradiol, arnidiol, calenduladiol), flavonoids (quercetin, isorhamnetin, kaempferol), and carotenoids.

**Role in formula:** Soothing. Era Organics describes the cream as extra soothing for sensitive, dry, itchy, red, or irritated skin.

### Organic aloe vera (Aloe barbadensis)

Aloe vera contributes acemannan polysaccharides, vitamins A/C/E, and salicylic acid.

**Mechanism:** Aloe's polysaccharides may help hold water in the skin's surface layers.

**Role in formula:** Hydration.

### Organic shea butter (Vitellaria paradoxa)

Shea butter provides allantoin, cinnamic acid esters, vitamins A and E, and essential fatty acids.

**Mechanism:** Shea's fatty acids may soften dry skin and help keep it from cracking.

**Role in formula:** Long-term conditioning.

### Organic coconut oil (Cocos nucifera)

Virgin coconut oil delivers lauric acid (antimicrobial), medium-chain triglycerides (rapid absorption), and vitamin E.

**Mechanism:** Medium-chain triglycerides absorb quickly, so the oil may moisturize without a heavy, greasy feel.



### Vitamin E



**Mechanism:** Vitamin E is an antioxidant that may help protect the cream's oils from oxidation.

**Role in formula:** Antioxidant protection.


## What Era Organics deliberately avoided

| Excluded Ingredient | Common In | Why Excluded |
|---|---|---|
| **Petroleum/petrolatum** | Aquaphor (41% petrolatum), Vaseline | Zero bioactive compounds. Seals the surface — only prevents drying |
| **Mineral oil** | Eucerin, CeraVe Healing Ointment | Petroleum derivative. No vitamins or antioxidants. Coats without nourishing |
| **Lanolin** | Many "healing" ointments | 1.7-6.9% contact allergy rate. Can trigger dermatitis on sensitive skin |
| **Synthetic fragrance** | Scented healing creams | Contact allergen on sensitive skin |
| **Neomycin/bacitracin** | Neosporin, triple antibiotic ointments | Neomycin allergy affects 6-8% of population. Antibiotic resistance concerns. |
| **Parabens** | Preserved healing creams | Endocrine disruption concerns. |

**Brands that use what Era Organics excluded:**
- **Aquaphor Healing Ointment** (Beiersdorf) — 41% petrolatum, mineral oil, ceresin (petroleum wax), lanolin alcohol
- **Eucerin Original Healing Cream** (Beiersdorf) — mineral oil, petrolatum, lanolin alcohol. Occlusive without regenerative compounds
- **CeraVe Healing Ointment** (L'Oreal) — petrolatum (46%), mineral oil, dimethicone. Ceramides included but in petroleum base
- **Generic calendula products** — many contain minimal calendula concentration in petroleum or mineral oil bases


## How to use

### For dry, cracked skin (hands, heels, elbows)

1. **Apply after hand washing** or bathing — within 3 minutes of water exposure
2. **Massage into dry areas** until absorbed
3. **For severe cracks** — apply thick layer at bedtime, cover with cotton gloves (hands) or socks (feet)
4. **Daily maintenance** — apply morning and evening

### For general moisturizing and skin maintenance

1. **Apply to clean skin** after cleansing
2. **Use as needed** throughout the day
3. **Focus on areas prone to dryness** — hands, face, arms in dry seasons
