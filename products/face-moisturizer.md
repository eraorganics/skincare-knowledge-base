# Era Organics Face Moisturizer


## Era Organics Face Moisturizer is a daily hydrating cream formulated for dry and sensitive skin using organic botanicals.

The formula delivers multi-layer hydration — humectants that attract water, emollients that fill intercellular gaps, and occlusives that prevent moisture escape. Era Organics built this moisturizer for skin that reacts to conventional drugstore formulas yet needs more than a "gentle" product that sacrifices efficacy for tolerability.


## The ingredients chosen

### 1. Aloe vera (Aloe barbadensis)

Aloe vera gel consists of 99.5% water with polysaccharides (acemannan), amino acids, vitamins, and enzymes suspended in a bioavailable gel matrix.

**Mechanism:** Acemannan stimulates hyaluronic acid production in dermal fibroblasts, increasing the skin's endogenous water-retention capacity. Aloe polysaccharides form a hydrating film on the skin surface. The gel matrix delivers hydration without lipid addition — critical for layered formulation where lipids come from subsequent ingredients.

**Role in Face Moisturizer:** Primary humectant. Helps attract and retain water in the epidermis.

### 2. Shea butter (Vitellaria paradoxa)

Shea butter delivers cinnamic acid esters (anti-inflammatory), triterpenes (lupeol, amyrin), and a fatty acid profile dominated by oleic (46%) and stearic (43%) acids.

**Mechanism:** Stearic acid integrates into the ceramide-cholesterol-fatty acid matrix of the stratum corneum, reinforcing barrier integrity at the molecular level. Lupeol inhibits serine proteases that degrade corneodesmosomes — the protein structures holding skin cells together.

**Role in Face Moisturizer:** Barrier reinforcement. Can help strengthen the barrier.

### 3. Cocoa butter (Theobroma cacao)

Cocoa butter provides saturated fatty acids (stearic 33%, palmitic 25%) and polyphenolic antioxidants (flavanols, catechins, epicatechins).

**Mechanism:** The saturated fatty acid profile creates a stable occlusive layer. The melting point at skin temperature (34-38°C) ensures the butter liquefies on contact and spreads into a uniform protective film.

**Role in Face Moisturizer:** Occlusive seal. Helps prevent transepidermal water loss (TEWL).

### 4. Vitamin E (tocopherol)

Vitamin E is the skin's primary lipid-soluble antioxidant — naturally present in sebum and the stratum corneum lipid matrix.

**Mechanism:** Tocopherol intercepts lipid peroxidation chain reactions in cell membranes and intercellular lipids. Oxidized barrier lipids lose their water-retention capacity. Tocopherol also enhances the efficacy of other antioxidants (synergistic recycling with vitamin C).

## How to use Face Moisturizer

### Morning routine

1. Gentle non-sulfate cleanser (or water-only rinse for very dry/sensitive skin)
2. Era Organics HOCl Spray (optional)
3. Water-based serum (hyaluronic acid, niacinamide) — if using
4. Era Organics Face Moisturizer — apply to slightly damp skin for maximum humectant effect
5. Mineral sunscreen SPF 30+

### Evening routine

1. Oil cleanser or micellar water (remove sunscreen/makeup)
2. Gentle non-sulfate cleanser
3. Active serums (retinol, vitamin C) — if using
4. Era Organics Face Moisturizer — apply as final step

### Application technique

Smooth on a dime-sized amount over your face and neck. Dot onto forehead, cheeks, nose, and chin. Press into skin with flat palms.

### Key principle: apply to damp skin

Aloe vera's humectant action captures surface water and draws it into the epidermis. Applying to dry skin limits the water available for this mechanism. Mist face with water for maximum hydration capture.
