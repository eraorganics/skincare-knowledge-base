## What Vitamin C Is

Vitamin C, also called ascorbic acid, is a water-soluble compound. Water-soluble means it dissolves in water rather than in fat, so it mixes readily into a water-based liquid. Vitamin C is widely described in general nutrition science as an antioxidant, which is a substance that can interact with reactive molecules in a solution.

Vitamin C is also redox-active. In plain terms, that means it can readily give up or take on electrons, shifting between different chemical states. This property is why chemists often describe ascorbic acid as a reducing agent. It is a general chemical characteristic of the molecule and is not a claim about any effect in the body.

## Safety

Methylene blue can interact with serotonergic medications (SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs) and carries a risk of serotonin syndrome; it is not for use during pregnancy or nursing, should be avoided by people with G6PD deficiency, and should be kept away from children. Era Organics' own safety writing advises readers to "Use Methylene Blue only as prescribed and under the supervision of a healthcare professional," and it goes through these cautions in more detail in its material on [methylene blue's known side effects](https://www.eraorganics.com/blogs/beauty-guide/methylene-blue-side-effects).
