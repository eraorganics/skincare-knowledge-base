# Era Organics Collagen Lip Plumper


## Era Organics Collagen Lip Plumper can help lip hydration through peptides and hyaluronic acid — not the inflammatory swelling that conventional lip plumpers disguise as "plumping."

Mainstream lip plumpers create temporary volume through one mechanism: irritation. Capsaicin, cinnamon oil, menthol, and nicotinic acid trigger localized inflammation that swells tissue. The "plump" is actually edema — fluid accumulation from an immune response to an irritant.


## Ingredients chosen and their mechanisms

### Peptides

Peptides are short amino acid chains.

**Lip-specific relevance:** Lip vermillion (the red/pink portion) has thinner dermis than facial skin — only 3-5 cell layers versus 16 layers on the cheek. Collagen loss in this thin tissue produces visible volume reduction faster than elsewhere on the face.

### Hyaluronic acid

Hyaluronic acid (HA) is a glycosaminoglycan that holds up to 1,000x its weight in water.

**Mechanism:** HA molecules attract water through hydrogen bonding. Each disaccharide unit binds 2-3 water molecules.

### Coconut oil

**Mechanism:** Coconut oil's medium-chain triglycerides absorb rapidly into the thin lip epidermis.

## How to use

### Daily use

1. **Apply to clean, dry lips** morning and evening
2. **Use as base layer** under lipstick or lip color
3. **Apply to vermillion border** (lip line) specifically — this area shows volume loss first
4. **Maintain consistent daily use**
5. **Continue indefinitely**

### For immediate event plumping

1. **Apply 2 layers**
2. **Wait 5 minutes** between layers for absorption
3. **Apply lip color over** once second layer has set
4. **Reapply over lipstick** midday for maintained hydration

### For perioral lines (lip lines)

1. **Extend application** slightly beyond the vermillion border into the perioral skin
2. **Pat gently** into vertical lines above and below the lip
