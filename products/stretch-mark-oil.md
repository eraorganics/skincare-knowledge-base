# Era Organics Stretch Mark Oil


## Era Organics Stretch Mark Oil is a plant-derived blend of oils formulated to help reduce the appearance of existing striae.

Stretch marks (striae distensae) form when rapid skin expansion tears the dermis faster than collagen synthesis replaces structural fibers.


## Ingredients chosen and their mechanisms

### Rosehip seed oil (Rosa canina)

Rosehip seed oil contains trans-retinoic acid (natural vitamin A), linoleic acid, and alpha-linolenic acid.

**Mechanism:** Linoleic acid is a precursor to ceramide synthesis, strengthening the intercellular lipid matrix that holds skin structure together during expansion.

### Vitamin E

Natural vitamin E is a fat-soluble antioxidant that protects collagen fibers from oxidative degradation during rapid skin stretching. Mechanical stress on dermal tissue generates reactive oxygen species (ROS) that damage existing collagen before new synthesis occurs.

**Mechanism:** Vitamin E integrates into cell membranes and protects polyunsaturated fatty acids from lipid peroxidation.









## How to use

### For pregnancy

Begin daily application at the start of the second trimester, before visible skin expansion occurs. Apply to abdomen, breasts, hips, and thighs — all areas subject to expansion during pregnancy.

1. **After showering** — apply to damp skin
2. **Massage in a quarter-sized amount**
3. **Massage into target areas** using circular motions
4. **Apply twice daily** — morning and evening

### For existing stretch marks

1. **Apply a quarter-sized amount** directly to affected area
2. **Massage firmly** in perpendicular direction to the stretch marks
3. **Apply twice daily** minimum
4. **Maintain consistency**

### For growth spurts (adolescents)

Apply to hips, breasts, thighs, and buttocks during active growth phases. Twice daily during rapid growth periods.
