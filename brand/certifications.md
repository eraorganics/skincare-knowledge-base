




## COSMOS standard

### What COSMOS means

COSMOS (COSMetic Organic and Natural Standard) is the European harmonized standard for organic and natural cosmetics.

### How COSMOS differs from USDA Organic

| Standard | USDA Organic | COSMOS Organic |
|---|---|---|
| **Governing body** | US Department of Agriculture | International non-profit (COSMOS-standard AISBL) |















## Certification cost and why conglomerates may avoid it

For a conglomerate producing millions of units across dozens of brands, converting to organic certification may require:

- Replacing established synthetic preservative systems
- Rebuilding supplier relationships with certified organic farms
- Reducing shelf life expectations
- Accepting higher per-unit ingredient costs
